Products Overview
K1 is a versatile interior/exterior waterproofing solution featuring elastic 
waterproof compound technology. Its thermal energy reflection and 
waterproofing capabilities deliver significant energy savings during summer 
months. Utilizing specialized pigments to prevent indoor temperature rise, 
K1 offers superior waterproofing and freeze resistance while strengthening 
building structures through enhanced durability and adhesion.
Unlike competitors who use two-part systems requiring separate mixing of 
base and hardener, K1 products feature convenient single-component 
formulation with all ingredients pre-mixed in one package. Additionally, K1 
is an environmentally friendly solution.
The product's core competitive advantage is its exceptional elasticity -
capable of covering large cracks and demonstrating up to 300% elongation 
capacity.
